Chess Morph Puzzle

Become the piece you step on. Tap to move as a rook, step onto a knight tile, and now you move like a knight. Slide, hop, and morph your way through 100 hand-tuned chess-piece puzzles.

How it plays

One clear rule. Every puzzle a question with a knowable answer.

Move

Tap any highlighted square to move as your current piece — rook, bishop, knight, queen or king.

Morph

Step onto a piece-tile to morph into that piece. The board you stand on rewrites the moves you can make.

Exit

Reach the glowing exit in as few moves as possible. Three-star ranks reward optimal solutions.

Craft

100 deterministic puzzles, every one BFS-verified solvable. No procedural filler — just puzzles.

Built for short, satisfying sessions

No timers. No nudges. Plays in portrait, one-handed.

Hint-and-undo jokers are earned by watching optional rewarded videos — never required. A puzzle game in the spirit of classic chess problems, but every piece you become is one you stepped on. The board teaches you.
