Make your move.

Tap a highlighted tile. Your current piece decides how you travel: straight lines, diagonals, or a knight’s leap.

02

Become something new.

Land on a dark piece to take its form. Each transformation can be used only once, so the order matters.

Find your way out.

No countdown, no opponent, no rush. Undo is always free and unlimited. Leave a puzzle and return to where you were.
